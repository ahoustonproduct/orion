# Core Analytics WashU Coverage Matrix

Source verified: 2026-05-20 from the official WashU Olin MS in Business Analytics curriculum page:
https://olin.washu.edu/programs/specialized-masters/ms-in-business-analytics/curriculum.php

This matrix maps Orion's Core Analytics rebuild to the WashU MSBA common core and the Financial Technology Analytics concentration. It is a preparation and support map, not a substitute for enrolled coursework.

## Program Snapshot

- Program: MS in Business Analytics.
- Total credits: 39.
- Common core: 18 credits.
- Concentration required courses: 12 to 13.5 credits.
- Electives, including required experiential course: 7.5 to 9 credits.
- Listed prerequisite: Statistics.

## Coverage Priorities

1. Keep the statistics prerequisite and common-core technical skills ahead of specialty finance depth.
2. Treat finance and fintech as natural data contexts, not a reason to skip the statistical concept.
3. Use deliverables to connect code, interpretation, limitations, and business communication.
4. Fill current partial gaps in this order: visualization polish, A/B testing project, text mining, prescriptive analytics, big data/cloud, then deeper concentration-specific finance math.

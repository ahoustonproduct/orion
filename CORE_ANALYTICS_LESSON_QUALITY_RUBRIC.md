# Core Analytics Lesson Quality Rubric

Use this rubric when grading rebuilt Orion lessons. The goal is not just more text.
The goal is better teaching.

## Score Each Lesson From 1 To 10

1. Depth of teaching
   - A 10 teaches like a compact textbook chapter with multiple examples.
   - A 5 introduces the topic but leaves the learner to infer too much.

2. Dependency chain
   - A 10 clearly builds from the prior lesson and prepares the next lesson.
   - A 5 covers a useful topic but feels disconnected.

3. Code and math integration
   - A 10 pairs Python objects with notation and hand-built calculations.
   - A 5 uses code or math, but not together.

4. Practice alignment
   - A 10 asks practice questions that directly rehearse the Learn section.
   - A 5 has practice questions that are only loosely related.

5. Challenge alignment
   - A 10 makes the learner code the same skill taught and practiced.
   - A 5 asks for a task that feels adjacent rather than cumulative.

6. Graduate tone
   - A 10 is serious, precise, and assumes Hack is preparing for a demanding MSBA.
   - A 5 sounds elementary or self-referential.

## Minimum Acceptable Lesson Standard

- Learn section is the longest section.
- Learn section has at least three worked code examples.
- Practice has at least 20 connected checks.
- Challenge has executable starter and model-answer code.
- The challenge tests calculation and labeled output.
- The lesson avoids app self-reference.
- The final bridge names what the next lesson will reuse.

## Questions For Hack

1. Grade each lesson from 1 to 10 after using it in the app.
2. Call out any section that feels too abrupt, too easy, or too thin.
3. Identify the first practice or challenge item that feels disconnected from Learn.
